A few answers before you start

Do I own the website?

The domain stays in your name. Éclora manages hosting in its own account. Once payment is complete, you receive your website files and the agreed access details. Third-party content remains subject to its licences.

What fees recur each year?

The launch hosting rate is $60 for the first year, then $120/year from the second year. Domain renewal is extra. No maintenance subscription is required; requested changes are billed individually.

Do I need to provide the text and images?

Yes. The package includes adding your content and making minor presentation adjustments. Full copywriting, translation and image purchases are quoted separately.

Can we add a newsletter?

Yes, as an optional extra with a quote. The sending service and any fees are specified before activation. Campaign writing and management are not included in the website creation package.

What is corrected after delivery?

Issues with our work reported within 10 business days of delivery, such as a button or form that does not work as agreed. New requests for content or design are paid changes.
